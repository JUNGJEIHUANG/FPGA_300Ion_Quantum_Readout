DW-ViT attains the highest reported $A$ and $E_{300}$ in every Table~\ref{tab:main_readout} column, including 99.2803\% all-frame site accuracy and 39.15\% all-300-state exact accuracy. Its 0.376M parameters and 0.415 GMACs support a compact FPGA-oriented realization, but these size figures are not lower than every neural baseline. The 219 unmatched detections reduce record-exact accuracy to 34.95\%, so the four reported state metrics do not by themselves establish uniformly better output records. On the 100 designated isotope-dark sites, DW-ViT is correct on 75, versus 100 for IPM and MN-2. The fixed-budget single-seed comparison does not establish statistical superiority, convergence, or multi-seed robustness.
